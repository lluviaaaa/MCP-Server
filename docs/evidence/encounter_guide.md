# Mire of Rot: Encounter Guide (party level 3-5)

**encounters.csv** is for random encounters while the party travels the swamp.
**loot.csv** is for what a defeated creature drops (source_creature shows the usual owner).

## How to roll
Roll d100 on a table. Each weight is the number of results that pick that row, counted down the table in order (weights total 100).
- Encounters: E01 1-20, E02 21-32, E03 33-44, E04 45-54, E05 55-64, E06 65-72, E07 73-80, E08 81-88, E09 89-95, E10 96-100.
- Loot: L01 1-20, L02 21-35, L03 36-50, L04 51-62, L05 63-72, L06 73-80, L07 81-88, L08 89-93, L09 94-97, L10 98-100.

## The three hazards (hazard_note column)
- **Quicksand:** the area has a hidden bog; a creature that enters sinks and needs help or a strength check to escape.
- **Acid rain:** a stinging downpour that damages unprotected creatures each round and ruins paper and cloth.
- **Toxic spores:** a cloud of spores that poisons anyone breathing it; covering mouth and nose helps.
"Hazard: none" means the encounter has no added hazard.
